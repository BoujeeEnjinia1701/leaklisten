# BOM notes

- Costs are indicative USD prices for one prototype logger, from typical distributor and hobby market prices in 2026. Every line is priced; supplier types are given where no supplier is chosen yet.
- Line numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Line 11 has no callout.
- Lines 12 and 13 were added and lines 1, 6 and 9 repriced when the design was made constructable (LKL-DDR-003, 2026-10-01): the strap hanger became a neck bar (line 9, USD 6 to USD 18), the logger housing gained turned flanged plugs, O-rings and radial screws (line 6, USD 12 to USD 16), the puck base was thickened for the magnet's stud (line 1, USD 8 to USD 9), and the internal chassis (line 12, USD 4) and the eye bolt and SMA bulkhead (line 13, USD 7) are new.
- Value-engineering target: USD 130 (`budget_usd` in `project.yaml`, a hypothetical control target, not a limit). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 142 per logger (USD 12 over the target), checked by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`, LKL-CAL-001 [M1].
- Changes at TRL 3: the seismic mass grows from about 11 g to about 53 g (line 3, +$1) and the puck from 34 mm to 42 mm tall, so the sensor meets its noise target on paper.
- Decided by Amish, 2026-09-25 (LKL-DDR-002): the 32 mm pot magnet ($5) is replaced by a 42 mm pot magnet with a keeper plate ($9), so the concept total rose from $109.00 to $113.00. The district gateway for iron covers is a TwinKit or partner gateway and is not in the logger's BOM; a through-cover antenna is fitted only where a utility agrees and is not in the total.
- Not included: a LoRaWAN gateway (TwinKit or a partner's network), the placement pole and cover lifting key (utility tools), and any network server costs.
- Decision of 2026-10-02 (LKL-DDR-003 follow-up): line 7 now includes the 3 mm clear acrylic status light pipe rod and its LED, USD 1.00 added (USD 30.00 to USD 31.00), which moves the total from USD 141.00 to USD 142.00. Basis: about USD 0.30 of acrylic rod, a share of a bag of clear epoxy and a status LED at distributor single-piece prices, rounded up.
